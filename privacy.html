Transit Rush — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Overview

Transit Rush is developed and published by James Morton. We respect your privacy and designed Transit Rush so that it can be played without creating an account or providing any personal information.

2. Information We Collect

No Personal Information. Transit Rush does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), and we never track you across other apps or websites.

Anonymous Gameplay & Usage Telemetry. To help us understand how players progress through the World Tour and improve level balance and app stability, Transit Rush uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service. TelemetryDeck collects only anonymized, aggregated product interaction signals—such as app session duration, level starts/completions/failures, booster usage, and anonymous salted device/app identifiers that cannot be used to identify you personally or track you across apps.

3. Local Device Storage

Transit Rush stores your game progress and preferences—such as your highest unlocked level, in-game coin balance, life regeneration timer, language preference, and sound/haptic settings—strictly locally on your iOS, iPadOS, or Android device using standard on-device preferences storage (UserDefaults / SharedPreferences). This local save data can be removed at any time by deleting the app.

4. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertising

Transit Rush does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, tracking pixels, or social media logins. Our sole analytics provider is TelemetryDeck GmbH, which processes anonymized usage signals strictly for first-party product analytics without cookies, persistent personal identifiers, or cross-site tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Transit Rush does not collect any personal information or persistent user-identifying data from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international privacy regulations for users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
