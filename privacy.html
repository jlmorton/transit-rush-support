Transit Rush — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

Transit Rush is developed and published by James Morton. We respect your privacy and designed Transit Rush so that it can be played completely offline without collecting any personal information from you.

2. Information We Collect

None. Transit Rush does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, or diagnostics.

3. Local Device Storage

Transit Rush stores your game progress and preferences—such as your highest unlocked level, in-game coin balance, life regeneration timer, language preference, and sound/haptic settings—strictly locally on your iOS or iPadOS device using Apple's standard on-device preferences API (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and can be removed at any time by deleting the app.

4. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertising

Transit Rush does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or social media logins.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Transit Rush does not collect any personal information from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international privacy regulations for users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
