Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Transit Rush?

Tap commuters who have an open path to the boarding curb so they can board the matching color bus. If a commuter's color doesn't match the currently active bus, they wait in the holding dock until their bus arrives. Be careful not to fill every dock slot without a match—that causes a gridlock!

How do special commuters and obstacles work?

As you travel through the World Transit Tour, you'll encounter Mystery (?) Commuters (whose color reveals when they reach the front), Linked Twins (pairs that board together and need 2 free dock slots if waiting), VIP Commuters, Tunnels, and Double-Decker Buses.

How do Coins, Hints, and Lives work?

Clearing city levels earns Coins. You can spend Coins in-game to unlock a temporary +1 Dock Slot, request a solver Hint, or refill your Lives. Lives also regenerate automatically over time. Transit Rush contains no real-money purchases or ads.

Where is my game progress saved?

All progress—including unlocked countries and cities, coins, and audio/haptic settings—is saved locally on your iPhone or iPad. No account or internet connection is required to play.

Privacy Policy Summary

Transit Rush does not collect, track, or share any personal data. All game state and settings are stored strictly locally on your device. No third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external servers are used.
